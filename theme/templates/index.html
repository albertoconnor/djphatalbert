The DJ Experience & Lessons

Whether you have never touched a mixer before or want to refine your transitions, get personalized 1-on-1 coaching behind real decks in a welcoming, fun environment.

Deck & Mixer Fundamentals

Get comfortable with the equipment: cue points, jog wheels, EQ sculpting, gain staging, and volume faders.

02

Understand rhythm, tempo matching, harmonic mixing, phrasing, and smooth blend transitions between your favorite songs.
